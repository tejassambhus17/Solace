# Explore practice redesign

The redesign keeps the existing Explore stack, practice IDs, authenticated services, and approved artwork. The visual direction pairs the existing serif typography with black and plum in dark mode, cream and forest in light mode, and lavender for current or selected states. Practice players retain the existing active clock, audio ownership, and saved Journey records.

## Evidence

The before build was `work/four-tab-sweep/final-web`. The after build was `work/nested-redesign/after-web`. Chromium rendered both against disposable authenticated records and a local offline-provider API. The matched pass covered 14 states at 320×660, 390×844, and 430×932 CSS px in both themes. A separate 390×844 pass covered generated Moment completion and the saved state in both themes. Each build passed 84 matched route checks and four completion checks. No uncaught runtime exceptions or horizontal document overflow were recorded. The completion test advanced the browser’s monotonic clock after a real start, pause, and resume, then verified persistence through the API.

The final integrated export at `work/nested-redesign/after-web-final` passed 14 targeted checks at 390×844 in both themes. Category filters showed the appropriate tools and practices, and the guided player restarted to its ready state without saving a completion. The mixer played two bundled WAV layers concurrently, paused both, accepted a keyboard-selected timer, and persisted the favorite. A generated personal plan saved, started, and reopened its practice. Mindful Mornings persisted its four-day enrollment. The final Moment pass again saved one completed record in each disposable account.
