## Petal prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent botanical illustration for a premium BLACK-mode wellness mobile app, Solace. Create one sculptural pair of delicate translucent petals, grown from the same tiny base, isolated on truly transparent background. Square 1024x1024 composition, whole subject visible with generous 15 percent breathing room. Petals curve gently upward in a refined leaf-like gesture, precise subtle natural veins, smoked lavender and pearl-champagne translucent material, restrained warm amber illumination from within the lower core. This image will be placed on pure black #000 and neutral charcoal #111112. Light must be gentle and dimensional, not harsh white; outer petals are smoky violet, inside honey light, elegant and calm. Museum-quality botanical macro art, photorealistic fine detail, no plastic, no cartoon, no grain. No green, no opaque background, no cast rectangular shadow, no broad white halo, no neon, stars, glitter, typography, logos, borders or UI. Preserve real alpha transparency.

## Stillness prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: quiet production editorial artwork for BLACK-mode wellness app's grounding practice. Landscape 1536x1024. A refined close still life: three naturally smooth dark basalt river stones in asymmetrical balance, a fine pool of water with one subtle ripple, and a single delicate dried botanical branch, positioned only in the RIGHT THIRD. Extreme soft grazing warm champagne light with a faint smoky lavender reflection. Main background exactly neutral near-black charcoal #111112, no green tint, no forest or visible horizon. LEFT TWO THIRDS are almost entirely empty flat charcoal negative space for separately coded ivory text. Very fine real mineral texture, natural material, cinematic stillness, understated luxury editorial photography. Beautiful but quiet, not a spa stock image. Keep silhouettes legible against black without strong bloom. No symbols, text, logos, UI, border, grids, neon, glitter or excessive fog.

## Night prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background artwork for BLACK-mode Solace app's sleep and reflection tools. Landscape 1536x1024. Refined sculptural moonlit botanical study: a thin crescent of warm pearl light behind two elegant matte charcoal leaves, very delicate champagne veins, softly folding midnight-lavender silk at the lower right. Compose the entire subject in RIGHT THIRD. LEFT TWO THIRDS are quiet almost flat neutral black charcoal #111112 negative space for separately coded ivory text. No blue or green wash across background. Intimate nighttime light, sculptural texture, highly detailed real materials, quiet editorial wellness art, no landscape, no landscape horizon, no house. Very subtle lavender in the material only, no neon or bright white glare, no stars or glitter. No text, logos, UI controls, frame or border.
